Questions

Is Billfold really free?

Yes. You can create and download unlimited invoices for free. Pro removes the footer and unlocks brand colors.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no signup. Drafts are saved in your own browser.

Is my invoice data private?

Yes. Everything runs in your browser. Your invoice details are never uploaded to a server.

How do I save my invoice as a PDF?

Click Download PDF, then choose Save as PDF in the print dialog. Only the invoice is printed.

Which currencies are supported?

US dollar, euro, British pound, Indian rupee and Japanese yen.
